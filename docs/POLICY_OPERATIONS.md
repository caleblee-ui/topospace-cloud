
# Safe Policy Operations

v3.5 turns online optimization into an enterprise-operable learning system:
- tenant/task policy persistence
- offline replay before promotion
- shadow challenger execution
- champion/challenger promotion guardrails
- automatic rollback on quality/latency/error regressions
- concept drift detection
- policy explanations for operators and audits

Learning policies should not be promoted solely because they reduce tokens. Quality, latency and risk
constraints remain first-class promotion criteria.
